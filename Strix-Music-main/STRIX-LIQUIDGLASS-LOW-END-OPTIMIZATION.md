# Strix Music — Liquid Glass low-end optimization

- Shared navbar glass keeps a single backdrop surface.
- Constant lens distortion is removed; lens is only evaluated while the active tab is pressed.
- Chromatic aberration is disabled for the navbar selection effect.
- Inner shadow radius/alpha are reduced.
- Existing Strix routes/icons remain unchanged.
- Navbar visual position is raised by 3dp from the previous -8dp offset.
- Media3's real MediaSession transport controls remain intact; notification is marked as public transport media.
