# Strix Music 9.20.0 — Visual Update

- Modernized bottom navigation with cleaner icons/highlights and animated selection.
- Removed the tab-switch visual blur effect; tab color transitions are short and crisp.
- Added optional iOS-style Glass Theme toggle in Look & Feel > Theme.
- Added optional Aurora Animation toggle in Look & Feel > Theme.
- Added animated Aurora background behind the app theme when enabled.
- Modernized the account/profile card with a premium gradient glass treatment and subtle motion.
- Kept thumbnail rendering untouched.
- Audio normalization remains enabled by default; loudness remains configurable in Player & Audio.

## Important
WhatsApp-related force-closes cannot be confirmed as fully fixed without an Android crash stacktrace/logcat from the device. This source update focuses on the UI/theme changes and keeps the playback service intact rather than guessing at a device-specific crash.
